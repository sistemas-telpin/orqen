## Delivery (`ENTREGA`)

For the lightweight and full lanes, orqen has archived the change and merged its deltas into `openspec/specs/`. The context identifies the archived change in `openspec.dir`. For the direct lane, refer to `orqen/work/<KEY>/` and `etapas_previas.ANALISIS`.

1. Read the development summary in `etapas_previas` and any archived proposal, specs, and tasks.
2. In `notas_release`, give the team technical release notes covering changes, migrations, flags, and risks.
3. In `mensaje_cliente`, summarize the delivered work and exclusions in language the customer uses.
4. In `pasos_validacion`, give the customer concrete validation steps derived from the scenarios or acceptance criteria.
5. Leave repository files as they are.
